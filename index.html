AI Schedule Assistant

Paste a Gemini API key from Google AI Studio (aistudio.google.com/apikey). It is saved in your database and only people who can edit the schedule can use it.

Google renames models from time to time. If the assistant says the model was not found, put the current Flash model name here.

Don't type personal information. Your message is sent to Google's Gemini service to read the schedule.
